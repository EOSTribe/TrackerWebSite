Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short: Tribal Task Tracker does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the Tribal Task Tracker app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login and no servers of its own. We have no way to see your tasks, your history or anything else you put in the app.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your tasks and their settings: names, icons, colors, notes, schedules, goals, durations, times of day and reminder times.
  • Your progress history: completed, skipped and timed days.
  • Your profile settings: the optional name you enter and your language choice.
  • Video links you add, and copies of video files you choose to attach to tasks.

This data is protected by iOS file encryption while your device is locked. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own backup settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Photos and Files

When you attach a video, you choose it yourself with the system Photos or Files picker. The app only receives the video you pick — it cannot see the rest of your photo library or files, and it does not ask for photo library access. The chosen video is copied into the app's private storage on your device so it keeps playing even if you delete the original. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders and timer alerts locally on your device. No notification content is sent through our servers or any third-party service. You can turn notifications off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Video links and other services

If you add a web link to a task (for example, a YouTube video) and open it, it opens in the YouTube app or in Safari. From that point, the website or app you open handles your data under its own privacy policy, such as Google's privacy policy for YouTube. We do not receive any information about what you watch.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing and selling of data

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share or sell any data with anyone.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You are in full control of your data. You can edit or delete any task inside the app at any time, and deleting the app permanently removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
